# Doca (ZenDoc)

**Your safe space for mental health — anonymous, accessible, private.**

Doca connects people with licensed therapists and doctors without revealing who they are. No names, no judgment, no barriers. Just help when you need it.

---

## What it does

- **Find a therapist anonymously** — Browse doctor profiles without using your real name
- **Book sessions** — Pay per session or subscribe, all tied to your anonymous alias
- **Wellness tools** — Breathing exercises, journaling, grounding, body scans — right in the app
- **Crisis detection** — If things get serious, the system escalates quietly to your doctor, guardian, or emergency services
- **Doctor dashboard** — Therapists manage their schedule, patient records (aliases only), and crisis alerts from their browser

---

## Who it's for

| Who | What they use |
|-----|---------------|
| **Patients** | Mobile app (iOS & Android) — join anonymously, self-guided or with a guardian |
| **Doctors** | Web dashboard — manage bookings, records, and crisis reviews |
| **Admins** | Web dashboard — approve doctors, audit compliance, oversee crisis events |

---

## Why it exists

Mental healthcare is hard to access. Stigma, cost, privacy concerns, and long wait times stop people from getting help. Doca removes the names, the paperwork, and the friction — so people can focus on getting better.

The platform is built from day one to:
- Keep identity separate from care
- Make crisis response fast and auditable
- Work on any device, anywhere
- Let doctors run their practice without overhead

---

## The big idea

A mental health platform where **privacy isn't a feature — it's the foundation.**
